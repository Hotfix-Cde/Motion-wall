# Contributing to MotionWall

Thanks for helping improve MotionWall.

## Before opening a change

- Keep the app simple and focused on local video wallpapers.
- Prefer small, understandable changes over unnecessary architecture.
- Avoid adding network services, accounts, analytics, or broad permissions unless there is a strong reason.
- Preserve Android 9+ compatibility where practical.

## Pull requests

Please include:

1. What changed.
2. Why the change is useful.
3. How you tested it.
4. Any Android-version-specific behavior or limitations.

For wallpaper changes, test both the in-app preview and the actual Android live wallpaper when possible.

## Bug reports

Include your Android version, device model if relevant, the video format involved, what you expected, and what actually happened. Screenshots or logs are helpful when available.
